# Chains 29 and 30: baked visual gate

**PASS for the blocking-defect gate.** Chain 30 improves the surface finish, vegetation variety, vehicle materials and street furniture overall. No new blocking trim hole, missing arena-facing building, lost waterfront landmark, broken vehicle material split or texture seam was observed. This is not a claim that every pixel improved: the darker blue vans have less body contrast in deep shade, and several inherited concept-fidelity defects remain below.

## Nonblocking backlog, ranked for follow-up

These are visible remaining deficiencies, not reasons to reject this integration. Cause assignments are hypotheses. Previously routed chain 31 repairs are not assumed present in this pinned chain 30.

1. **Shaded asphalt and blue vans lose separation.** Area 04 / Old Fulton: e04-front, gate12. Surface/lighting lane (%64 and lane C): asphalt palette, daylight/SSAO, baked indirect light and vehicle paint. Dark cool fill plus dark paint leaves lower body, rubber and road close in tone. New blue vans reduce body contrast relative to the pale baseline vans. [e04-front](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-03/chain30-audit/e04-front.png) · [gate12](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-03/chain30-audit/gate12.png)
