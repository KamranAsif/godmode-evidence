# BattleDuty-aimbot: FPS roguelite game-feel research

6 October 2026. For the Windows juice lane. Research and a quick static scan of `origin/main` on the Mac; no playtest. "Present" means a code path exists, not that its current mix feels good. All values prefixed **Try** are proposed defaults, not claimed measurements of reference games. Public talks and patch notes seldom disclose frame-perfect production values. Tune with the game's shake setting and a live playtest.

## Ranked implementation checklist

Let kill confirmation override plain hit, not stack three equally loud pings. **Try:** onset within one rendered frame of confirmed event; armor marker 180–250 ms; normal sound ±3% pitch, armor break fixed/pitched consistently. | **Partial:** four marker kinds at 140/240/320/560 ms in `scripts/hud_layout.ts`; `hit-confirm`, `kill-confirm`, `kill-thump` in `scripts/game_audio.ts`; `armor-break` in `scripts/reviewed_audio.ts` appears to be **player** armor only. |
| **2** | **Every hit changes the victim, not just the reticle.** DOOM's glowing stagger state clearly offers a Glory Kill and health reward; the signal is readable amid a horde. Avoid turning every hit into a full-body ragdoll. **Try:** flash 50–90 ms; flinch 100–180 ms; elite stagger window 0.6–1.0 s if gameplay permits. | **Partial:** Admin mesh hit/death treatments in `scripts/game_world.ts`; check actual visibility on DUMBO lighting. |
| **3** | **One unmistakable death beat.** COD kill confirm and feed, Battlefield kill feed, DOOM resource shower, and ULTRAKILL's escalating style rewards all give the final blow a second tier of response. [DOOM loop](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2020/01/21/how-doom-eternals-emphasis-on-variety-makes-it-a-bigger-better-sequel/), [ULTRAKILL creator interview](https://www.mclibre.org/descargar/docs/revistas/wireframe/wireframe-48-en-202103.pdf). | On death: thump + marker + visible body reaction + kill feed on the same event; special headshot/boss accent. **Try:** feed enters over 120–180 ms, holds 2.5–4 s; reserve 10–20 ms of audio priority for kill transient. | **Present:** kill confirm/thump, dedicated feed, boss marker. **Check:** synchronization and audio masking. |
| **4** | **Weapon report layers and acoustic tails.** Infinity Ward recorded/layered weapon sounds for weight, and DICE built shared gun/explosion components that adapt to environment and distance; a gun should sound different indoors, outdoors, and far away. [Infinity Ward audio interview](https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/2019-07/Modern-Warfare-Initial-Intel-Creating-an-Orchestra-of-Incredible-Audio-Effects-Weapon-Sounds-in-Call-of-Duty-Modern-Warfare), [DICE BF1 sound-team interview](https://www.asoundeffect.com/battlefield-1-sound/). | Give each player gun a dry close crack, mechanism, low body, and short waterfront/building tail. NPC rifles use attenuated distant report; occluded shots low-pass behind concrete. **Try:** 40–100 ms early reflection, 0.3–0.8 s tail; randomize non-identity layers ±2–4%, not the recognizable hit cue. | **Partial:** per-family cues, pitch ranges, distance/occlusion in `scripts/game_audio.ts`; likely needs mix and location-specific tails. |
| **5** | **Readable recoil, stable aim.** MW2019's animation direction emphasizes weapon weight and confident handling; MWII/MWIII are useful A/B references for how much visual disturbance is tolerable. [Infinity Ward animation interview](https://blog.activision.com/ja/call-of-duty/2019-07/Modern-Warfare-Initial-Intel-Detailing-Advancements-in-Animation-and-Authenticity), [MW2019 animator diary](https://www.infinityward.com/news/2020-04/DevDiary_RanonS). | Separate actual shot direction from viewmodel kick. One fast backward/up pose, slower spring settle; hip fire more travel than ADS, keep optic legible during bursts. **Try:** 35–55 ms kick, 90–160 ms return; horizontal randomness lower than vertical. | **Present:** per-gun hip/ADS travel and recoil pose in `scripts/weapon_feedback.ts`; inspect clipping and sight occlusion before adding more. |
| **6** | **ADS and sprint transitions communicate readiness.** MWIII's sprint delays ADS; tac-sprint is fastest but raises weapon and costs still more ready time. [Official MWIII movement guide](https://www.callofduty.com/guides/training/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-III-play-guides-multiplayer-movement). BO6 connects movement and reactions with animation [official reveal](https://www.callofduty.com/en/blog/2024/06/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-worldwide-reveal-announcement/). | Make crosshair/weapon/readiness agree, including interrupted sprint and reload. If adding tac-sprint, give it a distinct high carry silhouette and clear fire-ready delay, not just speed. **Try:** optic settles over last 60–100 ms of ADS; ready cue at the actual fire-ready frame. | **Partial/present:** ADS, sprint raise, holster/ready sounds in `scripts/reviewed_audio.ts`; no clear separate tac-sprint from quick grep. |
| **7** | **Reload has meaningful contact points.** MW2019 weapon animation work emphasizes practiced manipulation [animation interview](https://blog.activision.com/ja/call-of-duty/2019-07/Modern-Warfare-Initial-Intel-Detailing-Advancements-in-Animation-and-Authenticity). | Align mag-out, mag-in, chamber, ammo update and cancel windows to hand contact. During hordes, front-load motion and finish with a clean ready sound. **Try:** first contact within 150 ms, final settle 80–120 ms; preserve each gun's gameplay reload duration. | **Present:** staged sounds at 0%, 45%, 75% of duration in `scripts/reviewed_audio.ts`; check clip synchronization. |
| **8** | **Streak earn and call-in are separate peaks.** BO6 scorestreaks earn in increasing thresholds; UI tells players a new option is available [official BO6 equipment guide](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2024/10/call-of-duty-black-ops-6-launch-comms-equipment-perks-scorestreaks). | Earn: short radio chirp, bright slot pulse, peripheral banner. Call-in: hand/voice confirmation, world arrival, crowd response, then duck UI. **Try:** earn sting 300–500 ms; slot pulse 2–3 cycles over 0.5 s; no repeated earn sting while ready. | **Present:** killstreak system, slots and banners in `scripts/server/killstreaks.ts`, `scripts/client/survival_hud.ts`; polish event timing/priority. |

ULTRAKILL visibly names stylish plays [creator interview](https://www.mclibre.org/descargar/docs/revistas/wireframe/wireframe-48-en-202103.pdf). | On 3+ fast kills, boss kill, streak call-in or room clear: compact chat burst, viewer count pulse, optional TipBot line and donation stinger. Make reactions specific ("CLIP IT", "AIMBOT???") and rare enough to mean something. **Try:** chat burst 0.5–1.0 s after event, 3–8 lines; viewer pulse over 1.5–2.5 s; TipBot cooldown already 12 s, let nuke preempt. | **Partial:** chat/TipBot/viewers/tips in `scripts/client/stream_session.ts`; multi-kill callout in `scripts/client/roguelite_combat_juice.ts` is currently **log only** with another kill sound. |
| **10** | **Short hit-pause only on rare peaks.** Jonasson & Purho's [*Juice It or Lose It*](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1016487/juice-it-or-lose) and Nijman's [*Art of Screenshake*](https://www.jwaaaap.com/) demonstrate escalating feedback; Hades is a useful combat comparison. Their talks do not publish a universal FPS pause value. | Don't pause every automatic-rifle hit. Reserve client-only hit-pause for boss armor break, elite execution, final room kill; keep input, networking and audio live. **Try:** 25–45 ms at time scale 0.1–0.2 for elite; 60–80 ms only on room clear. | **Present:** room-clear hit-stop 80 ms at 0.05 time scale plus 600 ms beat in `packages/game-rules/src/roguelite_combat_juice.ts`. |

[Battlefield Labs destruction](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/news/battlefield-labs-destruction). | On DUMBO concrete, metal rail, glass, water and wood: distinct decal/particle/sound. | **12** | **Distance, occlusion and silence budget.** DICE's BF1 team describes different gunshot responses for forest, room and open field [interview](https://www.asoundeffect.com/battlefield-1-sound/); BF6 patch notes explicitly refine distant projectile and obstruction audio [EA update](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/news/battlefield-6-update-1-1-1-0). | Keep nearby enemies and incoming fire intelligible over horde ambience; duck ambient/music briefly for armor break, boss warning and TipBot donation. **Try:** 3–6 dB duck for 150–300 ms; max one foreground announcer/TipBot at once. | **Partial:** audio distance/occlusion and TipBot ducking exist; audit priority/masking. |

## Lower-priority reference notes

- **Battlefield 1 / BF3 / BF4 / 2042 / BF6:** borrow DICE's close/distant/environmental sound layering, squad warnings, surface language and restrained near-fire feedback. The [BF1 interview](https://www.asoundeffect.com/battlefield-1-sound/) explicitly traces the shared component approach back to BF3. BF6's [destruction post](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/news/battlefield-labs-destruction) is the clearest source for readable world damage. Full structural destruction is out of scope for a quick prototype; localized breakables would give most of the perceptual benefit.
- **MW2019 / MWII / MWIII / BO6 / Warzone:** compare weighty animation and reports against aim clarity. The [MWIII guide](https://www.callofduty.com/guides/training/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-III-play-guides-multiplayer-movement) makes sprint readiness tradeoffs explicit, and [Warzone patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/au/en/patchnotes/2024/11/call-of-duty-bo6-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) confirm separate armor break audio and hit marker configurations. Avoid claiming their private shake/reload timings.
- **DOOM 2016 / Eternal:** stagger glow and a reward on execution teach aggression. BattleDuty can reuse this for elite vulnerability and room-clear drops without copying the full Glory Kill camera lock. [id/PlayStation breakdown](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2020/01/21/how-doom-eternals-emphasis-on-variety-makes-it-a-bigger-better-sequel/).
- **Apex / Destiny 2 / Borderlands:** Apex's shield crack and numbers are combat-state UI; Destiny's recognizable crit/weapon sound and Borderlands' loot/numbers are useful A/B listening/visual references. Bungie describes audio as psychological cueing and weapon sound as a major production area in its [audio diary](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/51457). No verified public duration/pitch specification found for their crit cues; do not present fan measurements as facts.
- **ULTRAKILL / Risk of Rain 2 / Megabonk:** ULTRAKILL's style rank links varied high-skill actions to an escalating reward [creator interview](https://www.mclibre.org/descargar/docs/revistas/wireframe/wireframe-48-en-202103.pdf). Risk of Rain 2 layers item procs and a distinctive score; its sound team's [interview](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2020/12/interview_discussing_soundtracks_and_audio_design_with_the_skewsound_team) is useful for music/SFX coordination. Megabonk is a current visual comparison for screen-filling reward density, but I found no reliable developer timing breakdown, so use it as footage reference only.
- **Valve / classic principles:** [Jonasson and Purho's GDC session](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1016487/juice-it-or-lose) is the best staged demonstration that layers of particles, motion and sound transform the same game. [Nijman's talk index](https://www.jwaaaap.com/) and [contemporary summary](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/vlambeer-co-founder-shares-advice-on-building-better-action-games) emphasize directional, proportional shake. These are design principles; they do not justify adding full-screen shake to each bullet. I did not find a primary Valve talk with reliable FPS-specific numeric values in this pass.

## Camera and audio budget for this game

The **existing** damage camera shake is capped at `0.035 rad` (about 2.0°) and lasts `280 ms`, with player choices 100/50/0%. Room clear already punches FOV by 9° over 70 ms, returns by 600 ms, flashes to 0.35 alpha, and pauses client engine time for 80 ms. Those are BattleDuty values from `packages/game-rules/src/roguelite_combat_juice.ts`, **not reference-game values**. Suggested budget: keep normal weapon recoil mostly in the viewmodel; player damage may use the existing shake, but other effects should take its maximum rather than add to it; reserve the large FOV punch for clear/boss events. Maintain the 0% shake option and avoid flashes covering the reticle. The local hit cue should remain intelligible above gunfire; let streamer and streak sounds duck under the shot/kill moment. TipBot already has a 12 s gap and 4-item queue in `packages/game-rules/src/tipbot.ts`, which is a good anti-spam baseline.

## Fastest useful pass

Prioritize **1 → 2 → 3 → 9 → 4 → 8**. These expose hit state, make kills land, and use the stream fiction as a unique reward. Verify in one horde fight that an ordinary hit, armor break, headshot, kill, boss kill, streak earn and room clear can each be identified by sound alone and at a glance. Check the 0% shake option and make sure TipBot does not mask player danger cues.
