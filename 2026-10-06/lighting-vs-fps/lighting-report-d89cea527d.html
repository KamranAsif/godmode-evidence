Lighting investigation: FPS prototype versus roguelite

The roguelite has a valid directional bake, real-time sun shadows and SSAO. Its weaker depth is primarily a lighting and material contrast problem: strong ambient light and emissive material fill lift shaded details. The prototype also has different, more reflective materials and a compact covered garage. Copying its exposure makes the roguelite substantially worse.

Windows; AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT; GodotJS 4.6.1.stable.custom_build.14d19694e; Forward+ / D3D12. Ryzen 7 9800X3D; 32 GB RAM; Windows 10 19045.

Report only. No PR, merge or production lighting change. Scratch overrides and audit atlases were restored after testing.

Ranked causes and recommended fix

RankCauseEvidence and implication
1Ambient and emissive fill reduce shade separationAmbient energy is 1.5 versus 0.16 in the prototype. Faceted materials add shade_fill=0.5 to EMISSION. Reducing ambient alone deepens shop recesses, trim and props; shade_fill=0.2 modestly improves facade and ground contrast. These controls have no measurable consistent frame cost. Baked masonry responds less than dynamic trim; matching 0.16 blindly makes some details too dark.
2Different material response, geometry and lighting compositionThe prototype uses textured PBR concrete, wet floor patches, a specular sun, SSR, eight reflection probes, a bound VoxelGI and shadowed local fixtures. The roguelite has mostly matte faceted surfaces, no reflection probes, a lower specular sun, broad skylit streets and a different sun angle. SSR alone produces only a small change on the current rough street. The prototype exterior wall is also relatively flat. Individual probe/material contributions were not isolated, so this attribution is partly inference.
3Shadow bias and optional screen-space effectsSun normal bias is 1.4 versus 0.5. Lowering it gives a subtle contact change; SSIL adds a small improvement at about 0.3 ms. SSAO is already stronger than the prototype and disabling it removes useful contacts. Sun shadows are already Dynamic; disabling them flattens the scene while saving 4.66-11.24 ms.
4Tone mapping differs, but prototype exposure is unsuitable hereFilmic alone changes the street modestly. Filmic plus exposure 1.6 washes out the current morning palette, bleaching foliage and pavement. The existing ACES / 0.5 profile is not the main cause of weak shade separation.
5Lightmap density: low-priority hypothesis after matched bakeMatched 1 m versus 0.5 m cropped bakes keep the same 126 bound receivers, 1.75M placed triangles, 9283 probes, Environment, sun and materials. 106 receiver UV2 hints increased. Only small local differences are visible; contact depth and the faceted pavement pattern remain. Both use eight 4096 atlas layers. Frame changes are +0.01 ms outdoor, +0.27 covered, +0.06 contact; gameplay -0.32 ms is within live-run variance. This region does not justify a global density increase.

Recommendation: Start with the tested faceted shade_fill=0.2 and tune the active western_block_lighting ambient energy downward from 1.5; copying 0.16 makes some details too dark. Keep the Dynamic sun, SSAO, current ACES/exposure and general lightmap density. The combined candidate still needs visual review; only individual controls were tested. SSIL is optional at about 0.3 ms. If baked masonry remains flat, test the sky-to-sun ratio with a cropped bake before increasing density globally.

Distant uncropped receivers use the runtime crop path; neither atlas is a production replacement. Ordinary receivers <=256 m extent use 1 m in the control and 0.5 m in the density pass; existing 0.5 m hero metadata and larger charts retain their targets. Requested texels rise from 23,322,800 to 25,371,968 (+8.8%). Both atlases are 4096x4096x8, and both retain 9283 probes. The native control logged probe BSP triangulation warnings; probe interpolation is a limitation for dynamic objects. Repeated warning printing was disabled only around the denser native bake call, then restored; receiver, probe and runtime validation still pass. Bake wall-clock durations therefore should not be compared as density performance. FPS is measured independently in normal GodotJS runtime.
