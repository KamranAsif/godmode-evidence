# Desktop UI audit - round 1

Awaiting the external Mac auditor. No PR is open yet.

Branch: `codex/desktop-progressive-ui` - commit `1a96bcd44`.
Worktree: `D:/work/godmode-roguelite-worktrees/desktop-progressive-ui`.
Every linked PNG is an original 2560x1440 capture from `godot-cli game run --full --offscreen`, under a capture GPU hold. Existing fonts, colours and illustrations are retained. No paid APIs were used.

## Profiles and coverage

- Fresh: a new player's installed kit, no played streams, $0. The first-boot captures show the actual scripted install. Before that install, LiveTube owns the story opening; subsequent logins open BattleDuty with LiveTube closed.
- Mid: a synthetic six-stream save, 1,000 earned peak viewers, $900. Stream Boost is at 1% (bar only), Friendly Fire Off at 40% (requirement), Kevlar at 50% (picture), and Faster Regen at 80% (name and description). Below-1% feats are absent.
- Late: a synthetic 24-stream save, 100,000 peak viewers, $18,000, with purchased cheats and additional owned killstreaks. The final Front Page contract is not reached in this profile.
- Coverage includes login, desktop, default/maximized loadout, owned-streak picker, every revealed cheat category, upgrade groups and scrolled rows, all four LiveTube pages, messages, settings, controls, reset confirmation, and debug/performance panels. Duplicate top/bottom views occur where a short group fits without scrolling.
- These synthetic history fixtures have no saved gameplay thumbnails or per-second recordings. The channel/content pages truthfully show their empty-media states. No fake gameplay images were added.
- Debug/performance panels intentionally retain their developer tuning controls. LiveTube pages remove retention formulas and percentages; locked channel rewards use the same staged reveal as the game app.

## What changed

BattleDuty opens after login. START GAME lives inside LOADOUT; the gun's Change button and armour picker are removed. Locked cheats and unowned killstreaks no longer flood the collection/picker. Achievement cards reveal only their bar at 1%, requirement at 25%, picture at 50%, and name/description at 75%. Purchases still require their existing full unlocks. Upgrade details occupy a separate column; expanded groups reset their scroll so Back remains reachable. Stream-feature settings stay hidden until earned. Backdated opening history does not discover channel achievements.

## Two-line PR answer

UPGRADES is the shop for buying permanent cheats and higher ranks.  
CHEATS is the collection for inspecting your kit and unlocked cheats; a future combined Cheats screen with Shop/Installed views could make this clearer.

## Verification

`pnpm build`, `pnpm lint` (existing max-lines warnings only), and `pnpm format`; 37 targeted rules tests and 11 desktop unit tests passed. A headless desktop smoke loaded without script errors. The full suite, required opening playability check, and one kamran-reviewer round are reserved for the final approved change before opening the PR. No merge is authorized.
