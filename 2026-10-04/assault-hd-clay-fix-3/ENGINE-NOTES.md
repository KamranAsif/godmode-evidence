# Assault HD clay fix 3 — awaiting independent vision audit

15,129 uncropped 1920×1080 originals and 132 contact sheets cover 36 core weapon clips, four tablet phases and four supplementary rope-contact clips, each in FP, side and close-side views. This is a candidate for audit, not a visual PASS. Fixed-arm gameplay source and PR #936 remain unpublished pending the auditor's complete sign-off.

## Exact inputs and frame index

[recipe/core](recipe/core) and [recipe/added](recipe/added) retain production sources, capture fixture, clay assets and source commit records. [sampling.json](sampling.json) lists every sample time, native key and transition boundary. [frames-manifest.json](frames-manifest.json) pins original hashes. Per-clip [pose-receipts](pose-receipts) contain raw/corrected joint transforms, prop phases and contact residuals. [joint-metrics.csv](joint-metrics.csv) and [problem-frames.json](problem-frames.json) mark review leads; red contact-sheet borders identify these leads. Original images are unmarked. Unflagged metrics cannot certify visual contact or absence of mesh openings.

The original core recipe pins `92ec5f82c`. The branch then rebased onto announced main `a189ac508` as `999123ca3`; all 16 frozen production arm-render files remained byte-identical. All nine machine-gun clips were recaptured with #955's merged peep-opening/sight geometry, pinned in [recipe/machine-gun-refresh](recipe/machine-gun-refresh), including original asset hashes and the exact grey conversion. ADS replacements, tablet and supplementary rope clips use the rebased added recipe. The remaining core weapon frames retain their original core recipe. These recipe distinctions describe the actual captures, rather than attributing the original core to a later asset snapshot.

Core coverage is reload, ADS entry/hold, hip-idle, running, sprint entry, knife, swap, firing and firing-ADS for machine gun, pistol, shotgun and sniper. Every native animation key is included along with 60 Hz procedural samples and transition boundaries. Production WeaponView, first_person_pose and KnifeView apply the grip, wrist, fingers, carry and recoil; held ADS freezes native idle at source time zero. Sniper scope overlay, HUD, blur and muzzle flash are omitted to reveal underlying geometry.

## Rendering

Native Windows GPU Godot through godot-cli, windowed and off-screen. Uniform grey, no normal maps or textures, roughness 0.8, key light −35°/−35° at energy 1.3, ambient 0.28. FP has production FOV 50° and near plane 0.005 m. Side: (−1.9, 0.12, −0.45), target (0, −0.25, −0.45), FOV 45°. Close side: (−1.05, −0.08, −0.4), target (0, −0.28, −0.4), FOV 45°. Tablet close-side uses +1.05 X to expose the tapping index. Captures wait for the completed draw after pose publication, including descendant transforms and skin palettes.

Actual separate magazine/shell meshes and sockets are present. Tablet uses the approved model and actual glass plane; LAUNCH target is UI pixel centre (280,236) in 560×372, tablet-local (0,−0.01667,0.0014). Grey clay hides lettering, but preserves contact geometry.

## Diagnosis and candidate corrections

- **Wrist and elbow openings:** fix-1 holes were genuine exposed mesh boundaries under deformation. The high-resolution body was rigged directly on Mixamo; there was no weight transfer. Native mesh vertices, weights, UVs, bone lengths, 65 bones and 174 clips remain intact. Closed skinned inner cloth overlaps bridge glove/cuff and elbow/sleeve boundaries: 1,944 added vertices and 3,872 triangles total. The HD-only pipeline reproduces these additions; provenance pins the pristine compiled body and repaired outputs. Plain cloth bands and creases still require visual judgment, particularly at extended insertion contacts.
- **All weapon holds, ADS, gait, swap and recoil:** reported support-palm and elbow-plane flips were procedural. Re-deriving the elbow pole from each animated wrist caused discontinuities. The candidate instead uses the new rig's measured idle geometry and a stable native-derived pole. Shoulder depth derives from that rig's measured firing-hand position, with native segment lengths retained. Foreend reach is bounded by measured arm reach, palm seating uses the actual gun surface, and MCP/PIP/DIP curl blends with native finger articulation. Rifle/pump thumbs target the opposing exterior wall. Pistol cup uses measured idle hand separation and exterior receiver/thumb targets. Inspect all pad/thumb intersections and the original pop windows; changed sample indices must be matched by source time.
- **Reload:** native motion and procedural prop handling both contribute. Procedural holding fades during native two-handed motion, and late native upper-body motion blends toward idle over the final 12%. The old magazine detaches from MagazineSocket, is carried by the off hand, exits FP and is released; a fresh magazine spawns in that hand off-screen, enters FP and seats. Grasp closes before detach/spawn and opens before independent release. Finger curvature now uses opposed sidewall thickness instead of a cross-section diagonal. Thumb contact follows the actual carried magazine wall after wrist clamping, rather than the requested palm frame. Recovery returns to the ordinary support grip. Each of these contacts and transitions remains subject to individual-frame audit.
- **Shotgun reload:** shell orientation and palm placement were procedural contact defects. The actual shell cylinder now sits outside the palm, with its radius driving the opposing pinch. Only the shell advances into the tube socket; grasp is established before FP entry, then releases for insertion and recovers between shells. Three separate shells are shown. Inspect receiver clearance, external pad contact and recovery.
- **ADS rear geometry:** fix-1 used a rear-cut weapon variant that exposed hollow surfaces. HD uses complete weapon geometry and eye relief derived from rear overhang. Both transition clearance and sight framing need audit.
- **Knife:** reported recovery intersections were procedural carry/arc defects. The blade travels farther outboard and the primary stays down/away longer until knife hand and cuff clear. Two-camera contact inspection is required; silhouette overlap alone does not prove collision.
- **Tablet:** both HD hands grip the side bezels, thumbs oppose the front edge and the right index taps the real LAUNCH plane. Raise, hold, tap/recovery and lower are sampled through production tablet/hand classes. Lower retains the actual raised pose and clock instead of restarting a full-height animation.
- **Rope:** the off-hand fist wraps the returned cylinder contact frame while the primary firing arm uses its ordinary solve. These four supplementary weapon clips use the actual 1.2 mm FP rope radius and sustained production shot cadence, including native shot keys. They are contact diagnostics, not complete helicopter descent. Lane D's actual van/rappel/parachute N1 sets use its earlier 4b/db7 baseline and have separate immutable evidence pins; their known elbow opening remains under audit. Complete actual descent must pass independently, and this set cannot substitute for it.

Native-relative wrist correction is capped at 20° with a unit test; forearm roll has a separate 60° budget. Metrics distinguish the original reload clip from its intentionally blended late native pose. [Validation records](recipe/added/validation/validation.json): pnpm format ran, rebased pnpm build passed with client/server arena equivalence, lint passed with zero errors and six warnings, and project/diagnostic imports passed. Twelve wrist/finger/reload/reach tests passed; their production inputs remained byte-identical after rebase. A full-game off-screen survival smoke confirmed 65 Mixamo bones and accepted seven machine-gun reload phases; its owned instance was stopped. Gameplay had no script errors. Runtime inspection emitted Camera3D::unproject_position camera-plane errors from inspection_bridge.gd, so no warning-free inspection is claimed. No peer's build is used as this branch's validation.

Three directly inspected visual leads are marked even when metrics do not flag them: shotgun reload frame 0050's firing thumb-heel/cuff boundary in side-close, pistol sprint frame 0060's support-thumb/firing-hand clearance in FP, and tablet tap frame 0020's screen occlusion and index-pad/support-thumb contact. They require individual-frame comparison to distinguish openings or intersections from shading and projection, and to verify usable tablet framing. No claim of resolution is made from a single sample.
