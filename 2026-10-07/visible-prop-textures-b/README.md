# B-strength visible asset texture batches

Stacked on #1010. Kamran reviews the look and continuous playback before merge. No auditor verdict is claimed.

75 named models opt into the new material routes, plus procedural furniture, foliage, waterfront and building finishes. Existing B sedan/van/tree responses remain included for context. Two capture passes: 57 sheets at 095175a8c and 33 sheets at 9e017bd21.

Each contact sheet contains three unscaled 1920×1080 close/mid/far PNG panels, plus a label strip. Building panels are representative runtime materials, not extra authored models. Gallery views retain native lighting and physical metre scales; they are not matched production lightmap/contact proofs.

Batch 2 is the final source. Its updated moving-crate sheets supersede the batch 1 crate sheets. Batch 1 remains an immutable record of the first pass. The final six street views and tour include B-strength trim changes.

Tours join six native realtime camera paths by hard cuts. 1080p30 encoding holds/drops captured frames according to measured timestamps; no motion interpolation or resized pixels. Original section MP4s and timing receipts are included. Readback stalls of up to 554 ms (batch 1) / 534 ms (batch 2) limit smoothness; no shimmer PASS is claimed.
