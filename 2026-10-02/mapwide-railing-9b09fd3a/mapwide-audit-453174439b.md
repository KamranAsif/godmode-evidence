# Map-wide PR visual audit

Flat branch cc7aacfd: FAIL full flat-colour goal. Railing PR882 head 9b09fd3a: FAIL map-wide coverage. These supersede the earlier sheet-only scoped passes.

Rendered 22 flat-branch views and 21 railing-branch views with owned godot-cli offscreen instances at 1920x1080. No game/art changes committed; owned engines stopped. Flat branch was reviewed unbaked, as supplied sheets were. Railing branch was also reviewed unbaked after main’s atlas rejected its changed hierarchy. Original main reference images are baked; compare geometry and material features, not exposure differences. Final combined bake remains unverified.

## Blocking findings

1. **Flat surfaces: shared material cleanup is incomplete across the map.** The grid/seam cleanup generalizes to streets, parks and closures, but the shared shader still hashes cells, perturbs NORMAL, computes a random tone and multiplies ALBEDO by that tone. This is colour/normal variation in the material, not solely lighting on actual geometry. Especially conspicuous on broad roofs, facades, grass and bridge surfaces.

Evidence: [roof/wall overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-flat-cc7aacfd/c04-46f302cc75.png), [Water/Washington roofs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-flat-cc7aacfd/c01-53f95eadfd.png), [park and bridge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-flat-cc7aacfd/park-west-ff8043a4e4.png).

Owner/cause: assets/maps/western_block/faceted-surface.gdshaderinc lines45–58, dispatched by scripts/client/faceted_surface.ts and arena semantic overrides. The roof-slate shader was simplified, but the rendered arena still uses the shared patterned finish. Main’s bake also rejects changed branch tarp geometry: `Study geometry moved since baking: C10_L0_R0_106/ApprovedVisual/tarp-fence`.

2. **Railing: eastern shoreline coverage disappears.** The new model looks good at06/07, but formerly drawn eastern riverwalk rail is now mostly absent, leaving a raw exposed bank.

Evidence: [same-camera main/branch comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-railing-9b09fd3a/compare-gate01-386d2012fd.png), [east shore player-eye](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-railing-9b09fd3a/shore-01-44c0e247a1.png). Camera XZ: gate01(-222.04,-494.20); shore01(-251.49,-470.79).

3. **Railing: inner Pier1 channel loses long stretches of railing.** The overview and close bank view show missing replacement spans away from the approved cameras.

Evidence: [main/branch Pier1 comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-railing-9b09fd3a/compare-c05-6646d5ab9a.png), [inner-channel bank](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KamranAsif/godmode-evidence/main/2026-10-02/mapwide-railing-9b09fd3a/shore-07-82536364ef.png). Camera XZ: c05(-785,-200); shore07(-734.86,-184.99).

Owner/cause for2/3: scripts/environment_modules/waterfront_railing.ts, placeWaterfrontRailing. Candidate fits reject unsupported plates; `previous = post` and span creation only for two supported posts silently omit the railing when fitting fails. Ground extraction is in scripts/client/waterfront_railing.ts. This implementation is shared/map-wide, but its success is limited to supported stretches; do not patch only a camera’s stretch.
