# Assault rifle viewmodel verification

Every raw frame is 1920x1080. Sheets concatenate full frames without resizing. `game-` frames come from the full roguelite arena, pinned via the production runtime action; neutral ADS frames use the production renderer at weapon FOV 50 for an identical lighting/background comparison. The pre-#958 frame uses the exact 17c363ac5 mesh and socket-driven pose.

ADS: true size, 9 cm rear eye relief, weapon FOV 50 and world ADS FOV 45. Original #963 full stock outside ADS, with 25 mm eye clearance throughout hold/fire/reload. Finger and magazine/bolt/return corrections are baked in clip keys.

Build, format, lint, 19 rifle/pistol asset unit checks, import and headless smoke passed. `pnpm test`: 12/13 steps passed; existing debug-action source parser and WeaponView reload mock failures reproduced from origin/main. No new end-to-end suite.
